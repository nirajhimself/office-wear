# OFFICE WEAR — Business Foundation

## Positioning
Modern clothing for working professionals.

## Initial categories
- Shirts
- Trousers
- T-shirts
- Polos
- Overshirts
- Blazers
- Co-ord sets
- Essentials

## Initial assortment
Launch lean with approximately 10–20 SKUs. Prioritize repeatable essentials over catalogue breadth.

## Brand voice
Clear, confident, understated, useful and modern. Avoid hype, fake scarcity and inflated claims.

## Customer journey
Discover → collection → product → size → bag → checkout → delivery → review → repeat purchase.

## Launch principle
Start small, validate demand, improve the core experience, then expand assortment and AI capabilities.
