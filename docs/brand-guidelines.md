# OFFICE WEAR — Brand Guidelines

## Brand promise
**Clothing for the modern workday.**

OFFICE WEAR should feel premium without feeling loud: minimal, modern, confident, sophisticated, clean, editorial, professional, young, urban and timeless.

## Logo system
- The supplied OFFICE WEAR logo is the primary brand mark.
- Source asset: `/public/assets/brand/logo.png`.
- The production asset is cropped to its original non-transparent bounds only; no shape, lettering, color or proportions were changed.
- Never redraw, stretch, rotate, recolor or add effects to the logo.
- Keep clear space around the mark and never place it over visually noisy imagery.
- Use the light lockup treatment when the black logo sits on dark footer surfaces.

## Color tokens
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Black | `#090909` | Hero, manifesto, footer |
| Ink | `#101010` | Primary text and controls |
| Paper | `#F1F0EB` | Main storefront surface |
| Paper 2 | `#E9E8E2` | Collection/product sections |
| White | `#F7F6F2` | Light-on-dark type |
| Muted | `#73736E` | Secondary copy |

## Typography
- **Manrope:** editorial display headlines and large statements.
- **DM Sans:** navigation, body copy, labels and utility UI.
- Headlines use tight tracking and generous whitespace.
- Body copy remains short, readable and restrained.

## Layout
- Desktop content max width: 1412px.
- Global shell max width: 1480px.
- Navigation remains visually quiet.
- Sections use large vertical rhythm rather than boxed cards.
- Photography should occupy meaningful screen area.

## Controls
- Buttons are compact, uppercase and letter-spaced.
- Avoid pill-shaped controls unless the interaction genuinely needs one.
- Hover motion is subtle: opacity, small translation or image scale.
- No decorative gradients, excessive shadows or glassmorphism.

## Photography
- Prefer editorial, cinematic, high-resolution imagery.
- Natural texture and directional light are preferred.
- Avoid obvious stock-photo compositions.
- Product photography should be consistent in crop, lighting and background.

## Accessibility
- Maintain visible keyboard focus.
- Use semantic landmarks and meaningful alt text.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Maintain readable contrast.
